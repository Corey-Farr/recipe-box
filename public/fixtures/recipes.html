Lemon rice & crispy chickpeas

Ingredients · 4 servings

  • 1½ cups rice
  • 2 (14 oz) cans of chickpeas, drained
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • ½ cup chopped parsley
  • salt to taste

Cook the rice

Rinse the rice. Add 2¼ cups water, simmer covered for 15 minutes, then rest for 10 minutes.

Crisp & finish

Fry the drained, dried chickpeas in oil for 8–10 minutes. Fluff the rice with lemon and parsley; top with chickpeas.
